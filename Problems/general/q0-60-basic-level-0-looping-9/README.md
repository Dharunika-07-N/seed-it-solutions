## 📝 Problem Statement

### Example 1

**Input:**
```text
6 5
```

**Expected Output:**
```text
1 * 5 = 5
2 * 5 = 10
3 * 5 = 15
4 * 5 = 20
5 * 5 = 25
6 * 5 = 30
```

### Example 2

**Input:**
```text
4 2
```

**Expected Output:**
```text
1 * 2 = 2
2 * 2 = 4
3 * 2 = 6
4 * 2 = 8
```
